Flowdoku is a Sudoku game intended for people aged 13 and older. Version 1.0 offers Classic and Daily puzzles, themes, and optional one-time Remove Ads and Theme Pack purchases. This policy explains local game information, advertising, purchases and support.

Information kept on your device

Your puzzle boards, pencil notes, move history, unfinished games, Daily progress, XP, statistics, settings and selections are saved locally. A player name is generated on your device; you can change it and select one of the built-in avatars. The name and avatar are local profile preferences: they are not a public profile and are not sent to our servers or purchase/advertising providers by the game's profile feature.

Flowdoku does not require a Flowdoku account or provide a developer-operated cloud-save service. Game code does not upload these save files. Your operating system may include app information in its own device or cloud backups under your settings. Clearing local data or uninstalling the app may remove progress; a backup may restore it. Purchase restoration is separate from restoring game progress. An optional local performance-diagnostics file is not uploaded by the game.

Advertising and privacy choices

Flowdoku uses Google AdMob for rewarded continue ads and full-screen ads after puzzles. Google's advertising services may process IP-derived approximate location, identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information for ads, measurement and security. The precise handling depends on configuration, permissions and privacy choices. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks advertising privacy requirements. Where offered, the in-game privacy-preferences option lets you revisit your choices. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency permission is required before accessing the advertising identifier for tracking. Android provides advertising-identifier controls in device settings. Refusing personalised ads or tracking does not necessarily stop every ad or all service processing. These controls vary by region, device and operating system version; not every control is available to every user.

Remove Ads is not a promise to disable all advertising SDK processing. In the current implementation, privacy checks, SDK initialisation and ad preloading can still occur after that purchase. Removing ad displays must not be read as ending every connection to Google.

Purchases

The App Store or Google Play handles payments. Flowdoku does not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat supports purchase validation, entitlement delivery and restoration, using purchase/transaction information and an SDK-generated anonymous app user ID, with technical information needed for that service. Flowdoku does not send the local player name or avatar to RevenueCat and does not set a custom account identity or advertising-attribution integration in this implementation.

Purchase information is used for app functionality and purchase analytics. Current RevenueCat use is not configured to track you across other companies' apps for advertising. It is separate from Google's advertising processing. RevenueCat's Privacy Policy explains its processing. Uninstalling Flowdoku does not automatically erase store or RevenueCat transaction records. Restore Purchases requests eligible purchases through your store account; it does not restore locally saved puzzles.

Support, retention and deletion requests

When you email support@heltrum.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, secret keys or account credentials.

Local game data remains on your device until removed, replaced or affected by your device/backup settings. Support email is handled using Google Workspace. Support correspondence, including attachments, is deleted no later than 12 months after the last message in the correspondence. This period applies to support email, not to independently held store or purchase-provider records. Deleting the app does not automatically delete those records.

You can contact support@heltrum.com with a privacy or deletion request. We will assess the information we hold and the steps available through our service providers, subject to applicable requirements. Stores, Google and operating-system backup providers also have their own privacy and deletion controls; we cannot promise to erase independently held records on their behalf.

Providers, hosting and your rights

Advertising, purchase, email and website-hosting providers may process information outside your country. Their policies explain their own practices. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to a competent authority. Contact support@heltrum.com to make a request.

The privacy/support website uses GitHub Pages. Visiting that website is separate from local gameplay; its host may process connection information. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. The site uses no added analytics, advertising or submission form.

Collection methods, recipients and legal bases under Turkish law

Advertising and purchase information is collected electronically through the relevant SDKs and store requests. Support information comes directly from your messages and attachments; website connection information comes from visiting the hosted site. Local profile and puzzle files are not uploaded by the game's save/profile features.

Google and its advertising partners process advertising information; RevenueCat processes purchase validation and entitlement information; Apple or Google operates the store payment flow; Google Workspace handles support email; GitHub hosts the website.

For processing under Turkish Law No. 6698, we distinguish the following legal bases by purpose:

  • Necessary purchase delivery/restoration and directly related contractual support: Article 5(2)(c), performance of a contract.
  • Handling statutory privacy requests and necessary legal records: Article 5(2)(ç), compliance with a legal obligation; establishing, exercising or protecting a right: Article 5(2)(e), where applicable.
  • Necessary security/fraud-prevention and website-operation processing: Article 5(2)(f), legitimate interests, only where necessary and without overriding fundamental rights and freedoms.
  • Non-essential advertising, personalisation and measurement/analytics: Article 5(1), explicit consent where required; consent may be withdrawn through the available controls.

These bases do not make all SDK activity necessary for a contract. Providers may process relevant information outside your country. The receiving provider and purpose depend on the service described above; no specific transfer agreement or contractual safeguard is asserted by this policy.

Your rights under Article 11 of Law No. 6698

Where applicable, you may ask whether your data is processed, obtain information about that processing, learn its purpose and recipients, request correction or deletion subject to legal conditions and notification of those actions to recipients, object to an adverse result based exclusively on automated analysis, and seek compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Requests may be submitted in writing to the address at the beginning of this policy, or using other methods allowed by Turkish law. Contact support@heltrum.com for privacy questions and guidance. Ordinary email is not automatically a valid formal application in every case; applicable identity and submission requirements still apply. We assess requests within statutory time limits. Audience and changes

Flowdoku is intended for ages 13+ and is not directed to children under 13. This audience statement is not an age-verification system or a claim that 13 is the legal consent age everywhere. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact us so we can review the situation.

We may revise this policy when features or processing change and update its version date. For privacy questions, contact support@heltrum.com.
